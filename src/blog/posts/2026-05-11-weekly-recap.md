**Week 1 of Season 132** did not ease in. It announced itself.

POAP KINGS opened the new season with a **first-place finish** in Race 1 — 10,000 fame, rank 1 of 5, and a 2,800-fame gap over second place. The clan did not just win. It pulled away. **jaat @123** and **Atternam** both ran all 16 decks for **3,600 fame** each — the ceiling for the week. **Shafith Nihal** was right behind at 3,250 across 16 decks, and **Sandeep** and **Aaqib Javed** both cleared 3,000. Five players in the top tier. That is not a narrow win. That is a statement.

Twelve members are currently tracking as Iron King candidates with perfect attendance through four battle days: **jaat @123**, **Atternam**, **Shafith Nihal**, **Sandeep**, **Aaqib Javed**, **Vijay**, **King Thing**, **²⁸**, **Fullboat**, **Tere**, **Waltadr**, and **Th15_Guy**. The season is young, but that list is long — and every name on it will be remembered at the end.

The trophy board had serious movement this week. **²⁸** posted the biggest climb with **+1,500**, jumping from 5,500 to 7,000. **Mya Tun Kyaw** — six days into the clan — added 848. **xian** gained 607. **Ditaka** pushed past 10,500. And the battle pulse was on fire: **Cycle God**, **TR**, and **WARLORD** all ran perfect 10-0 streaks over their last 10 battles. Three separate players, all locked in at the same time. The energy is real.

Four new members joined this week — **Luc_nfr**, **jaat @123**, **Mya Tun Kyaw**, and **Aaqib Javed** — and at least two of them are already deep in the war standings. That is the best kind of welcome. Season 132 is one race in, and POAP KINGS is exactly where it should be.
