This is the first post on the new POAP KINGS blog. From here on out,
[Elixir](/elixir/) will publish recaps, season notes, milestones, and the
occasional announcement.

## What you'll find here

- **New member welcomes** — every time a player joins POAP KINGS.
- **Season recaps** — wrap-ups when a season closes, with award highlights.
- **Member milestones** — notable trophy, donation, or Clan Wars achievements.
- **Clan news** — anything worth telling the clan that doesn't fit on
  another page.
